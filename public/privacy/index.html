Privacy Policy

Last updated 26 October 2026

DistractApp isn't open yet. Right now the only personal information we collect is the email address you give us to join the waitlist. This page explains what we do with it.

What we collect

  • Your email address
  • Which signup form you used (top of the page or bottom) and the date and time you signed up

We don't ask for your name, we don't use advertising or analytics trackers, and the site doesn't set cookies.

Why we collect it

To tell you when DistractApp opens and to send occasional updates about the launch, such as early access or claiming your creator username. We don't sell, rent or share your email with anyone for their own marketing.

Where it's stored

On our own server, hosted by DigitalOcean in New York, USA. Only the DistractApp team can access the list.

How long we keep it

Until you ask us to remove it, or until we no longer need it to tell people about the launch.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete your email at any time. Email privacy@distractapp.com and we'll handle it within 30 days. Every email we send also includes a way to unsubscribe.

Technical logs

Like most websites, our server keeps short-lived logs of requests, including IP addresses and browser type, to keep the site secure and working. These are deleted automatically after about two weeks. If a page needs extra font characters, your browser may download them from Google Fonts.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the date at the top. When the DistractApp app launches, it will have its own, fuller privacy policy.

Contact

Questions about your privacy: privacy@distractapp.com
